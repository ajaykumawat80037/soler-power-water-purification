Sunlight can make dirty water safe to drink.

Over 2 billion people drink water that may be contaminated. Solar purification needs no fuel and no electricity bill, only sun and a few simple materials. Here is how it works and how much you can get.

Three ways to purify with the sun

Each method suits a different need: a family bottle, a household supply, or a village system.

SODIS (bottle method)

Clear PET bottles sit in the sun. UV-A light and heat kill bacteria, viruses and parasites.

Best for: drinking water for one person or family.

Cost: nearly zero

Solar still

Sunlight evaporates water under a glass or plastic cover. Vapour condenses and drips into a clean tray, leaving salt, metals and germs behind.

Best for: brackish or salty water, a few litres a day.

Cost: low

Solar PV + filter / UV / RO

A solar panel runs a pump with a filter, UV lamp or reverse osmosis membrane. Add a battery to work after sunset.

Best for: schools, clinics, villages.

Cost: medium to high

SODIS: how long to leave the bottle?

Pick the sky conditions to see the minimum exposure time.

6 hours

Bottle in full sun, laid on its side on a reflective or dark surface.

Steps

  1. Choose the bottle. Use a clear PET bottle up to 2 litres. Avoid scratched, coloured or glass bottles.
  2. Pre-filter if cloudy. Water must be clear enough to read print through the bottle. Strain through cloth first.
  3. Fill and shake. Fill three-quarters, shake 20 seconds to add oxygen, then fill completely.
  4. Expose to sun. Lay it on a tin roof or reflective sheet for the time shown above.
  5. Drink from the bottle. Pour into a clean cup or drink directly, to avoid recontamination.
